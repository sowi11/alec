About the Event

The HCDC Cross Blazers Cup 2027 is a fictional campus celebration that brings Holy Crossians together through sports, technology, creativity, and friendly competition. This event aims to develop students' talents, promote teamwork, strengthen friendships, and encourage sportsmanship. All HCDC students are invited to participate and represent their respective departments and teams.
